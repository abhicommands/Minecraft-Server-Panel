# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Minecraft Server Panel welcoming, safe, and
equitable for everyone. We respect the dignity, rights, perspectives, and
contributions of people of every identity, background, experience level, and
ability. Everyone participating in good faith receives the same privileges of
participation.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- engage kindly, honestly, and constructively;
- respect different viewpoints, experiences, and clearly stated boundaries;
- take responsibility for their words, actions, and technical contributions;
- give and accept actionable feedback gracefully;
- credit sources and protect confidential or personal information; and
- work to repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualization, intimidation,
threats, deliberate endangerment, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated unwanted
attention are not permitted. Neither are knowingly misleading reports,
uncredited copying, spam, or commercial promotion unrelated to the community's
purpose.

These restrictions cover direct behavior as well as encouraging, threatening,
or irresponsibly amplifying it.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation privately to the project maintainer,
[@abhicommands](https://github.com/abhicommands), using a private contact method
listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private method is available,
open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to establish private contact; do not
include names, evidence, or other sensitive incident details in that issue.

Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical. The maintainer
may review relevant records, request additional information, restrict contact,
remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit participation, suspend an
account from project spaces, or permanently ban a participant. The response
will reflect the severity, impact, context, and pattern of the behavior. No
specific response time is promised.

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report or assists an
investigation is itself a violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, release channels, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies
when a person officially represents the project in public.
